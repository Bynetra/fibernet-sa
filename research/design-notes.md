# Design and asset notes

## Direction

Warm, human home-internet branding: deep ink teal, restrained mint, soft ivory, generous whitespace and clear offer comparisons. Manrope for English and Tajawal for Arabic. The business's original fiber fan is redrawn in SVG to stay crisp at mobile and desktop sizes.

All original package entries are retained behind provider tabs. The original long promotional paragraphs are consolidated into an introduction, practical benefits, connection steps and FAQs. City links become a useful coverage enquiry form. Every plan action opens a WhatsApp draft for the correct plan; no automatic sending takes place.
